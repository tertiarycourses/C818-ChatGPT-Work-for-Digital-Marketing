# Lab 04 — Adapt and review the flyer variants

**C818 · Topic 2 · 55 minutes · Version 1.0**

## Goal

Produce square and story derivatives that retain the offer and remain readable.

## What you’ll build

creative/sourdough/flyer-square.pptx, flyer-story.pptx, their PDF or PNG proofs, variants-proof.md and design-review.csv.

## Prerequisites

Lab 3 A5 source and text proof. A layout editor and a browser or image viewer.

## Steps

### 1. Inspect the A5 proof

Open flyer-a5.pdf and flyer-copy.md. List the facts that must survive in each derivative: course identity, duration, fee, schedule, campus and one CTA. If Lab 3 stalled, use its checkpoint source.

### 2. Create separate canvases

In your layout editor create a 1:1 square canvas and a 9:16 story canvas. For editable PowerPoint use custom sizes 20 × 20 cm and 18 × 32 cm. Export raster images at 1080 × 1080 and 1080 × 1920 when the tool supports exact pixels; otherwise use PDFs and record their aspect ratio.

### 3. Request adaptation

Attach the A5 source or PDF, copy and ledger to Work, then paste prompts/task.txt. Ask for recomposition rather than cropping. Keep the academy colour and type rules.

### 4. Recompose the square

Put the headline and course title near the top, use a short details block, and give the CTA its own region. Do not shrink the whole A5 page to a square screenshot.

### 5. Recompose the story

Keep key information clear of the top and bottom interface zones. Use a conservative 10 percent top and bottom content margin for this classroom brief. Preserve a readable course identity and CTA.

### 6. Inspect at practical sizes

Open the square at thumbnail size and the story at a phone-width view. Read every factual line. Check text contrast; for normal screen text use 4.5:1 and for qualifying large text use 3:1 as the minimum check. Record the colour pair and tool/method used in design-review.csv.

### 7. Proof both variants

Transcribe all visible text for each format into variants-proof.md. Compare it with the A5 proof and selected-course.json. Record exact file names and any corrected line.

### 8. Export and hand off

Save editable sources and proofs in creative/sourdough. Fill assets/design-review-template.csv with at least six concrete checks and a status for each. Resolve every factual defect before proceeding to Lab 5.

## Task prompt

Copy `prompts/task.txt` into the Work task. Review the plan before authorising production.

```text

Adapt the reviewed A5 flyer to a square and 9:16 story format. Recompose the content for each format; preserve the BAK-101 identity, source-checked offer and academy brand. Keep one CTA and readable classroom draft wording. Return editable sources when supported, PDF or PNG proofs, a full text transcription for both variants and a review log covering source fidelity, crop, hierarchy and readability. Use no fake QR code, new offer or live booking promise.

```

## Test it

- Square and story have the requested aspect ratios, with essential text visible.

- Both variants match BAK-101’s title, S$680 fee and four-week duration.

- The story’s key text is outside the classroom top/bottom margin zones.

- design-review.csv records at least six reproducible checks and corrections.

## Troubleshooting

**The story crops the CTA:** Reposition the CTA within the centre content area; do not rely on automatic fit.

**Exported pixels differ:** Use a supported image export or keep an aspect-correct PDF and note the pixel-export limitation.

**A variant changes a fact:** Copy the approved factual text block again, export and recheck the entire transcription.

## Challenge

Create an accessible text-only companion that contains the offer and action for someone who cannot view the graphic.

## Reflection

Which elements needed recomposition rather than simple resizing?
