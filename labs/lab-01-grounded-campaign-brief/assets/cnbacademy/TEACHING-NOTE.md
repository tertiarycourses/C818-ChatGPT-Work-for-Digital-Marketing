# Cook & Bake Academy teaching website

This is the existing fictional Cook & Bake Academy catalogue reused for C818. It contains 20 synthetic courses (10 Bakery and 10 Cooking). Its fees, intakes and locations are scenario facts, not real offers. There is no verified cafe/barista course in this catalogue; do not invent one. Bakery skills provide the cafe-business audience angle.

Serve from this folder with `python3 -m http.server 8080` and open http://localhost:8080. The sign-up form stores demo entries locally in your browser; use only invented names and example.com addresses. Images load from the original Unsplash URLs and need an internet connection. The assistant uses the supplied local catalogue/knowledge files. Do not submit real enquiries or enter personal information.

Source reused: the existing Tertiary Infotech Cook & Bake Academy lab website. Snapshot: 1 October 2026. All marketing deliverables in this course remain classroom drafts.

The C818 copy preserves the catalogue and visual system. Its optional assistant has been adapted to catalogue-only lookup, with all API credential entry and network model calls removed.
