# Cook & Bake Academy — market brief

**Positioning:** Hands-on cooking and baking, taught by working chefs, in
classes small enough to get real help.

**Personas**
1. *Weekend home baker* (28–40): wants sourdough and pastry skills,
   books Saturday mornings, decides on a phone.
2. *Career changer* (30–45): testing a food business idea, pays for
   longer courses (French Foundations, Viennoiserie).
3. *Gift buyer* (25–50): buys one-session workshops and vouchers.

**Launch five:** BAK-101 Sourdough, BAK-102 Viennoiserie, BAK-104
Macarons, CUL-203 Sushi, CUL-210 Knife Skills.

**Pricing notes:** one-session workshops sit mid-market; the 8-week
courses are premium and need the small-class promise stated clearly.

**The website must:** show fee, weeks and schedule on every card; let a
visitor sign up in under a minute on a phone; answer allergy and parking
questions without a phone call.
