# C818 labs

Eight connected labs use the existing fictional Cook & Bake Academy website. Each folder has its own guide, prompts, assets and recovery checkpoint. Begin with Lab 1 and keep the same campaign workspace throughout. Detailed procedures belong in these guides and the Learner Guide.

## [Lab 01 — Create a grounded campaign brief](lab-01-grounded-campaign-brief/README.md)

**45 minutes · Topic 1**. campaign/brief.md, campaign/selected-course.json, campaign/claim-ledger.csv and campaign/unknowns.md. These define the facts used by every later asset. Produce a source-checked campaign brief for one bakery course. The source-checked output becomes input to the next lab.

## [Lab 02 — Build a reusable marketing workflow](lab-02-reusable-marketing-workflow/README.md)

**50 minutes · Topic 1**. campaign/workflow.md, campaign/brand-check.md and campaign/workflow-trial.md. The playbook carries the same rules into flyer, social and email production. Create and trial a source-grounded production playbook for the campaign. The source-checked output becomes input to the next lab.

## [Lab 03 — Design an editable bakery flyer](lab-03-flyer-design/README.md)

**75 minutes · Topic 2**. creative/sourdough/flyer-a5.pptx or another editable layout source, flyer-a5.pdf, flyer-copy.md, image-prompt.txt and text-proof.md. Create an A5 flyer with source-checked copy and a complete text proof. The source-checked output becomes input to the next lab.

## [Lab 04 — Adapt and review the flyer variants](lab-04-flyer-variants-and-review/README.md)

**55 minutes · Topic 2**. creative/sourdough/flyer-square.pptx, flyer-story.pptx, their PDF or PNG proofs, variants-proof.md and design-review.csv. Produce square and story derivatives that retain the offer and remain readable. The source-checked output becomes input to the next lab.

## [Lab 05 — Write a social article and platform packages](lab-05-social-article-and-repurposing/README.md)

**70 minutes · Topic 3**. content/article.md, content/instagram.md, content/facebook.md and content/editorial-review.md. Create a useful article and two short-form adaptations from the same approved facts. The source-checked output becomes input to the next lab.

## [Lab 06 — Build a content calendar and editorial gate](lab-06-content-calendar-and-quality/README.md)

**60 minutes · Topic 3**. content/calendar.csv, content/posts.md and content/content-review.csv. Create a five-post calendar with sources, dependencies and review status. The source-checked output becomes input to the next lab.

## [Lab 07 — Prepare a segmented email campaign](lab-07-email-sequence-and-segmentation/README.md)

**85 minutes · Topic 4**. email/sequence.md, email/email-1.html, email/email-1.txt, email/segment.csv and email/send-checklist.md. Build an unsent three-email sequence for an explicitly eligible synthetic audience. The source-checked output becomes input to the next lab.

## [Lab 08 — Measure and hand off the campaign](lab-08-measurement-and-campaign-handoff/README.md)

**50 minutes · Topic 4**. campaign/metrics.csv, campaign/recommendation.md, campaign/manifest.csv and campaign/handoff.md. Interpret synthetic results and package a source-checked, reviewable campaign handoff. The source-checked output becomes input to the next lab.
