## About the course

This practical course turns one verified campaign brief into editable flyer designs, useful social content and a segmented email draft. Learners review facts, preserve the academy brand, interpret synthetic metrics and hand off files that another marketer can inspect. The package follows the requested ChatGPT Work marketing scope; see [source and scope notes](SOURCE-NOTES.md) for the current registration-page mismatch.

## Learning outcomes

- LO1: Delegate a grounded marketing task in ChatGPT Work and maintain a source-checked campaign brief.

- LO2: Create and verify editable flyer designs and format variants using the academy brand.

- LO3: Produce useful social articles, platform packages and a coherent content calendar.

- LO4: Prepare a segmented email sequence, interpret synthetic campaign metrics and hand off a reviewable package.

## Topics covered

- ChatGPT Work and a grounded brief

- Flyer design and visual review

- Social articles and content planning

- Email campaign and measurement

## Using the package

Read the Learner Guide before starting. Lab 1 includes the existing academy catalogue; serve its cnbacademy folder with the command given in its README. Web learners upload the selected sources; desktop learners may use an approved local project. Image generation and skill installation are optional, with a source-file workflow and typographic recovery samples supplied. Detailed procedures stay in the LG and labs.

The lesson plan totals 450 instructional minutes plus 30 minutes of tea breaks per day, excluding lunch. Its delivery timetable is 09:30–18:30; the public page displays a different timetable, which needs operational confirmation before class scheduling.

## Public and private distribution

This repository publishes the current deck, Lesson Plan, Learner Guide and complete learner-facing lab tree. Source references in reference/ and any assessment/ materials are private and excluded, along with credentials, dependencies and superseded archives. This non-WSQ package contains no formal assessment. All academy facts and contacts are fictional teaching data; campaign assets remain classroom drafts and no messages are sent or scheduled.
